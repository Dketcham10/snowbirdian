Partner Program

Don't like management fees? Let's partner instead.

If your home earns more, we earn more. Every partnership is set up in writing with clear expense rules and monthly statements you can review.

Profit Share

No management fee. We take an agreed share of net profit after expenses, so slow months cost you nothing extra.

Base + Performance

A small base fee, plus a share of revenue above a target we set together. We win big only when you do.

Equity Partnership

Buying a property? You invest, we operate, and we own it together through an LLC built for the deal.
