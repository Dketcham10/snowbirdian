Privacy Policy

How we handle information you send through this website.

Snowbirdian Hospitality (“we,” “us”) operates www.snowbirdian.com. This page explains what the quote form collects, how submissions are processed, and how tracking works on the site.

What the quote form collects

When you use the quote form, you may provide:

  • Full name
  • Email
  • Phone
  • Property address
  • Bedrooms / sleeps
  • Plan of interest
  • Listing status
  • Start timeline
  • How you heard about us
  • Optional partnership details: who you are, investment range, and preferred structure
  • A free-text message

Required fields are marked in the form. Empty optional fields are not sent.

Where submissions go

Quote form submissions are sent to HubSpot, which we use to store contacts and follow up about your home. HubSpot processes that information on our behalf as our service provider.

HubSpot tracking cookies

We use HubSpot’s tracking script so HubSpot can record page views and help us understand traffic sources associated with a contact. That may involve cookies or similar technologies placed by HubSpot. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How we use the information

We use quote information to respond to your request, schedule a walkthrough when appropriate, and send related follow-up about Snowbirdian Hospitality services. We do not sell your personal information.

Deletion and questions

To ask us to delete your information, or for privacy questions, email contact@snowbirdian.com.
